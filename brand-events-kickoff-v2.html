Brand Events Kickoff Tool v2 · server-powered

Fill in the project details, pick the documents you need, and the server creates each one as a real Google Doc in your Google Drive — copied from the blank template and pre-filled with your details. Save each into your own project folder afterwards.
How to use this tool
  1. Fill in your project details in the form below.
  2. Select the documents you need.
  3. Click Generate — each document will be created in your Google Drive pre-filled with your project details.
  4. Open each document using the links provided and save into your project folder.
  5. Complete each document as your project progresses.
  6. At the end of your project, complete the Project Wrap Document within one week of your event wrapping.
